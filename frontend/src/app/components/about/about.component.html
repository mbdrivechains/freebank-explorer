easynet Explorer

This site shows the blocks, transactions, mempool and mining of easynet. Amounts are in {{ unit }}, easynet's test coin; the small unit is the szat.

easynet is a public test chain on eCash rules, mined with CPUs from its own genesis block. Its coins have no value, and the chain may be reset. It is for testing wallets, mining and drivechain withdrawals, and the ASERT difficulty rule: from block 2017 the difficulty changes with every block.
