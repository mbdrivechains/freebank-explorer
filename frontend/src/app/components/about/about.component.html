FreeBank Explorer

This site shows the blocks, transactions and mempool of the FreeBank sidechain. Amounts are in {{ unit }}.

FreeBank is a drivechain sidechain of eCash. Its blocks are not mined with proof of work. They are made by blind merged mining (BMM): an eCash miner commits the hash of each FreeBank block in an eCash block, for a fee. So FreeBank has no hashrate or difficulty of its own.
