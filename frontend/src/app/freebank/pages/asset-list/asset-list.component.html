Assets

Anyone can create an asset on FreeBank: a token with a ticker, a headline and a fixed supply, carried by ordinary coins (one unit per szat of the coin's value). Its creator holds a control coin. Tickers are not unique, so an asset is known by its id, the transaction that created it. A token is only as good as its issuer.
