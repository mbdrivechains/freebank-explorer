Bills of exchange

A bill of exchange is a written order to pay a fixed sum of ECX at a set block. The drawer writes it, the acceptor signs to pay it and locks an escrow bond behind it, and whoever holds the bill at maturity is paid. Holders pass bills on by endorsement, and a house can buy one before it falls due, paying in its own notes: that is discounting, and the bill then sits on the house's loan book.
