eCash ⇄ FreeBank

FreeBank's coins are backed by eCash held in FreeBank's treasury on eCash. A deposit from eCash moves eCash into the treasury, and a FreeBank block pays it out here, less 1,000 szats. A withdrawal to eCash burns FreeBank coins; eCash pays them out in a withdrawal bundle once eCash miners approve it.
