Pools

A pool is a market in one house's notes against ECX. It holds both and prices them by their ratio: every trade moves the price, and a small fee on each trade stays in the pool for the people who supplied it. At par one note unit is worth 1 szat, the sum the house redeems it for; the pool price shows what the market pays for that house's notes today.
